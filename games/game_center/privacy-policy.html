Privacy Policy for Artivy Game Center

Effective Date: September 26, 2026

Artivy Game Center is a free, ad-supported Android app from Artivy that holds seven puzzle games in one place: 2048, PawDoku, Kata·Word, StackO!, Rekta, PourFect! and Railo!. This page explains what the app keeps, what it sends, and who it is shared with. It covers this app only — each of the standalone Artivy games has its own policy, and they do not collect the same things.

1. Information We Collect

The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own. Nothing you type is sent anywhere. What it keeps and what it sends are different lists.

Kept on your device only, in Android's app preferences:

  • your progress in each game — levels reached, the board you are part-way through, best scores, streaks and statistics;
  • your points, what you have unlocked with them (themes, and the "remove ads" item), your achievements and which of them you have claimed;
  • how often you open each game, which decides the three games offered when you long-press the app icon;
  • your settings: sound on or off, and the theme you are wearing;
  • the year of birth you gave when the app first opened (see Children's Privacy below);
  • a switch that turns adverts off on this device, used for taking screenshots.

Android's Auto Backup is on, so these preferences may be copied to your Google account and restored when you set up a new device. That copy is held by Google under your account, not by us.

Sent off the device, by Google's own SDKs rather than by any server of ours:

  • your device's advertising ID and technical details about the device, requested by Google Mobile Ads to serve and measure adverts;
  • the analytics events and crash reports described next.

2. Analytics and Crash Reports

The app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, both from Google. Analytics records exactly these events and nothing else of ours:

  • that the app was opened;
  • game_open — which of the seven games was opened;
  • round_end — which game, whether the round was won, and how many seconds it took.

Crashlytics records the technical details of a crash: the stack trace, the device model and the Android version. Neither is used to identify you. Both are processed by Google under its own privacy policy.

3. Points, Unlocks and Achievements

Points are earned by playing, by claiming achievements, and by watching optional adverts. They are spent inside the app on hints, themes and similar unlocks. Points cannot be bought with money, have no cash value, and cannot be transferred or exchanged outside the app. There are no in-app purchases of any kind. Points, unlocks and achievements are stored only on your device.

4. How Your Information Is Used

  • to keep your progress, points and settings where you left them, on your device;
  • to show adverts, which is what pays for the app;
  • to find and fix crashes;
  • to see which games are played and how rounds go, so difficulty can be judged from something other than guesswork.

Your information is never sold.

5. Advertising Consent and Choices

The app shows adverts through Google AdMob. There are no banners. It shows:

  • a full-screen advert after every third round ends — a level solved, a run lost or a board reset, counted across all games — and never within ninety seconds of the last one;
  • rewarded adverts, which are always optional. A dialog names the advert and what it gives before anything plays: points in the Store, double points for a finished round, a hint when you do not have the points for one, or a second chance after losing. Declining costs nothing.

Where the law requires it — in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland — a Google consent form appears before the first advert is requested, asking whether adverts may be personalised. That choice can be changed at any time from Ad privacy options in the app's Settings screen, which appears wherever Google reports it is required.

6. App Updates

When a newer version is available on Google Play, the app asks Google Play to install it before you continue. This runs inside the Google Play Store app and sends nothing to us.

7. Third-Party Services

These services see data through the app and have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics

8. Children's Privacy

Artivy Game Center is built for children as well as adults, and we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

The first time you open the app it asks what year you were born, before any advert is requested. The answer is kept on your device, it is never sent to us, and it is asked only once. It decides one thing:

  • Players aged 13 or under are treated as children by Google's advertising SDK. Their adverts are not personalised, and no advertising identifier is used to profile them. Because a year of birth cannot tell whether a birthday has passed, anyone who might still be twelve is given this treatment.
  • Players over 13 see adverts as described above.

The question changes nothing about the games. Separately, and for every player regardless of age, advert content is capped at Google's “G” rating — suitable for general audiences, and sensitive advert categories are excluded.

9. Your Rights

Everything the app keeps is on your own device. Clearing the app's data, or uninstalling it, removes it — along with your progress, points and unlocks, which cannot be recovered afterwards. Advertising choices can be changed from Ad privacy options in Settings, and your Android advertising ID can be reset or deleted from your device's own settings.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy is updated when the app changes what it collects. The effective date above is when the current version took effect.
