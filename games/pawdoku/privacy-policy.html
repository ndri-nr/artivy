Privacy Policy for Pawdoku

Effective Date: July 28, 2026

Welcome to Pawdoku, a cat-themed logic puzzle game published by Artivy. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information is involved when you play Pawdoku. Pawdoku is a single-player, offline game: we do not require an account, and we do not run our own servers that collect your personal data.

Playing in a browser

There are two ways to play Pawdoku, and they do not collect the same things. Everything else on this page describes the Android app from Google Play. The browser version at artivy/pawdoku/play.html is narrower:

  • The level you have reached, the paws and marks on the board in front of you and your remaining lives are kept in your browser's local storage on the device you played on. They are never sent anywhere, and there is no way for us to read them.
  • Google AdSense is loaded on every page of this website, including the game. No advert is placed in the browser version yet, so you will not see one — but the AdSense script does run, and Google may set cookies or use similar identifiers under their own privacy policy. AdMob, described further down, is the Android app's advertising and is a separate thing.
  • There is no analytics and no crash reporting in the browser version.
  • The browser version has no coins, no store, no trophies and no hints or paws, so nothing on this page about purchases, rewarded adverts or consumables applies to it.
  • The browser version has no daily challenge and no timer, so nothing on this page about a daily puzzle, a daily streak or a calendar applies to it. It is the endless levels only.
  • Clearing your browser's site data for this domain erases your level. So does playing in a private window, or in a browser that blocks storage — the game will still run, but nothing is kept.
  • Progress does not follow you between devices, or between the browser and the Android app. They are separate saves.

1. Information We Collect

Pawdoku itself does not ask for, collect, or transmit any personally identifiable information about you. The only data involved is:

  • Game Progress (stored on your device only): Your current level, hearts, trophies, win and daily streaks, coins, hint and paw tokens, unlocked and selected themes, daily challenge results and solve times, statistics, and preferences are saved locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to Artivy.
  • Advertising Data (collected by Google): Pawdoku shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect device information such as a device/advertising identifier, IP address, and app-interaction data. This collection is governed by Google's own privacy policy, not by Artivy.
  • Crash Reports (collected by Google): Pawdoku uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to record diagnostic information if the app crashes (such as device model, operating system version, and the technical state at the time of the crash), so we can fix bugs. This is processed by Google in line with Google's privacy policy.

2. How Your Information Is Used

The information above is used only to:

  • Save and restore your game progress on your device.
  • Display interstitial and rewarded advertisements that keep the game free.
  • Diagnose and fix crashes so the game stays stable.

Uninstalling the app removes the game progress stored on your device.

3. Advertising Consent

Where required by law — for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom — Pawdoku shows Google's consent form before requesting personalised ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. If you were asked for consent, an Ad privacy options link appears at the bottom of the home screen, beside Privacy Policy and Terms, so you can review or change your choice at any time. Players outside those regions are never shown the form and see no such link.

4. Third-Party Services

Pawdoku uses Google AdMob to display advertisements and Google Firebase Crashlytics for crash diagnostics. Google may collect and process data as described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not use any other advertising networks or analytics providers.

5. Children's Privacy

Pawdoku is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Because Pawdoku shows ads via Google AdMob, we rely on Google's ad settings and family policies to help keep advertising appropriate.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at legal@artivy.id.
