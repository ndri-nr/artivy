Privacy Policy for PourFect!

Effective Date: August 22, 2026

Artivy built PourFect! as a free, ad-supported game. This page explains what it collects, what it does not, and what leaves your device. It covers both the Android app and the browser version on this site, because they do not collect the same things.

Playing in a browser

There are two ways to play PourFect!, and everything else on this page describes the Android app from Google Play. The browser version at artivy/pourfect/play.html is narrower:

  • Your level number and the board in front of you are kept in your browser's local storage on the device you played on. They are never sent anywhere, and there is no way for us to read them.
  • Google AdSense shows one banner advert in the browser version, below the board and clear of it. It is a plain display advert: it is not rewarded, it gives you nothing in the game, and there is no full-screen or between-levels advert anywhere in the browser version. The advert loads once when the page opens and is never refreshed while you play. Google may set cookies or use similar identifiers to serve it, under their own privacy policy rather than ours. AdMob, described further down, is the Android app's advertising and is a separate thing.
  • There is no analytics and no crash reporting in the browser version, so none of the events listed below are recorded when you play in a browser.
  • There are no rewarded adverts in the browser version. Nothing is locked behind one: taking back a move is free, and the extra bottle from level 7 is simply an ordinary bottle rather than something to unlock.
  • Clearing your browser's site data for this domain erases your progress. So does playing in a private window, or in a browser that blocks storage — the game will still run, but nothing is kept.
  • Progress does not follow you between devices, or between the browser and the Android app. They are separate saves.

1. Information We Collect

PourFect! has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own. Nothing you type is collected, because there is nothing to type. What the app keeps and what it sends are different lists.

Kept on your device only, in Android's app preferences:

  • the number of the level you have reached;
  • the board you are part-way through, so backing out of the app does not throw a half-solved level away, along with the moves behind it for the undo button;
  • whether the extra bottle on the current level has been unlocked, and how many adverts are still owed for it;
  • a switch that turns adverts off on this device, used for taking screenshots.

Android's Auto Backup is left on, so those preferences may be copied to your Google account and restored when you set up a new device. That copy is held by Google under your account, not by us, and it is what lets your progress follow you to a new phone.

Sent off the device, by Google's own SDKs rather than by any code of ours:

  • your device's advertising ID and technical details about the device, requested by Google Mobile Ads to serve and measure adverts;
  • analytics events and crash reports, described next.

2. Analytics and Crash Reports

PourFect! uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, both from Google. Analytics records how the game is used — such as levels started and finished, and whether a level was left unfinished — so that difficulty can be judged from something other than guesswork. Crashlytics records the technical details of a crash: the stack trace, the device model and the Android version. Neither is used to identify you, and neither collects anything you have typed, because there is nothing to type.

These reports are processed by Google under its own privacy policy. Nothing about the boards you solve is combined with anything else, and there is nothing to combine it with — the game has no account.

3. How Your Information Is Used

  • to keep your progress where you left it, on your device;
  • to show adverts, which is what pays for the game;
  • to find and fix crashes;
  • to see which levels people stop on, which is how the difficulty curve gets corrected.

Your information is never sold.

4. Advertising Consent and Choices

PourFect! shows adverts through Google AdMob: a banner on the Settings screen, a full-screen advert when you leave a finished level, and optional rewarded adverts for taking back a move or unlocking the extra bottle. The first five levels show none at all.

Rewarded adverts are always opt-in. A dialog names the advert and what it buys before anything plays, and declining costs nothing.

Where the law requires it — in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland — a Google consent form appears before the first advert is requested, asking whether adverts may be personalised. That choice can be changed at any time from Ad privacy options in the game's Settings screen, which appears wherever Google reports it is required.

5. Third-Party Services

These services see data through PourFect! and have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics
  • Google AdSense, for the browser version only

6. Children's Privacy

PourFect! is built for children as well as adults, and we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

The first time you open the Android app it asks what year you were born, before any advert is requested. The answer is kept on your device, it is never sent to us, and it is asked only once. It decides one thing:

  • Players aged 13 or under are treated as children by Google's advertising SDK. Their adverts are not personalised, and no advertising identifier is used to profile them. Because a year of birth cannot tell whether a birthday has passed, anyone who might still be twelve is given this treatment.
  • Players over 13 see adverts as described earlier in this policy.

The question changes nothing about the game. Every level, every bottle and every undo is the same whichever answer you give.

Separately, and for every player regardless of age, advert content in PourFect! is capped at Google's “G” rating — suitable for general audiences, and sensitive advert categories are excluded.

The browser version is a different case, and it is not aimed at children. It asks no age question, shows one ordinary display advert served by Google AdSense under Google's own policies, and reads nothing about who is playing. The age gate and the child treatment described above belong to the Android app.

7. Your Rights

Everything the game keeps is on your own device. Clearing the app's data, or uninstalling it, removes it — along with your progress, which cannot be recovered afterwards. Advertising choices can be changed from Ad privacy options in Settings, and your Android advertising ID can be reset or deleted from your device's own settings.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy is updated when the game changes what it collects. The effective date above is when the current version took effect.
