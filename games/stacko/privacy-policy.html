Privacy Policy for StackO!

Effective Date: August 7, 2026

Welcome to StackO!, a neon isometric block-stacking game published by Artivy. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information is involved when you play StackO!. StackO! is a single-player, offline game: there is no account, no sign-in, and no Artivy server that collects your personal data.

This website

Everything else on this page describes the Android app. This page itself is served from the Artivy website, which loads Google AdSense on every page. That is separate from the app's advertising: it is the website's, not StackO!'s, and it collects nothing on Artivy's behalf. Google may set cookies or use similar identifiers under their own privacy policy.

There is no browser version of StackO! yet. If one is published, this page will describe what it stores and what it shows before it goes live.

Playing in a browser

There are two ways to play StackO!, and they do not collect the same things. Everything else on this page describes the Android app. The browser version at artivy/stacko/play.html is narrower:

  • Your best score is kept in your browser's local storage on the device you played on. It is never sent anywhere, and there is no way for us to read it.
  • Google AdSense shows one banner advert in the browser version, below the board and clear of it. It is a plain display advert: it is not rewarded, it gives you nothing in the game, and there is no full-screen or between-levels advert anywhere in the browser version. The advert loads once when the page opens and is never refreshed while you play. Google may set cookies or use similar identifiers to serve it, under their own privacy policy rather than ours. AdMob, described further down, is the Android app's advertising and is a separate thing.
  • There is no analytics and no crash reporting in the browser version.
  • The browser version has no gems, no shop, no themes, no ranks and no continues, so nothing on this page about purchases or rewarded adverts applies to it. It is the Classic mode only — there is no Speed run and no Precision run.
  • Clearing your browser's site data for this domain erases your best score. So does playing in a private window, or in a browser that blocks storage — the game will still run, but nothing is kept.
  • Progress does not follow you between devices, or between the browser and the Android app. They are separate saves.

1. Information We Collect

StackO! itself does not ask for, collect, or transmit any personally identifiable information about you. The only data involved is:

  • Game Progress (stored on your device): Your best score in each mode (Speed, Classic, Precision), gems, lifetime statistics (games played, blocks stacked, perfect drops, best perfect streak, gems earned), owned and selected themes, and your options such as sound mute, haptics, and last played mode are saved locally in a settings file on your device. This data is not sent to Artivy. The "RANKS" screen is a personal tier calculated from your own local records — it is not an online leaderboard.
  • Device Backup (handled by Android): That same file is included in Android's app backup and phone-to-phone transfer, so your progress can be restored on a new device or after a reinstall. The copy lives in your Google account and is handled by Google under its own terms; Artivy does not perform the backup, receives nothing from it, and cannot guarantee a restore.
  • Advertising Data (collected by Google): StackO! shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect device information such as a device/advertising identifier, IP address, and app-interaction data. This collection is governed by Google's own privacy policy, not by Artivy.
  • Analytics Data (collected by Google): StackO! uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is used in aggregate — for example how often it is opened and which screens are viewed. This is processed by Google in line with Google's privacy policy.
  • Crash Reports (collected by Google): StackO! uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to record diagnostic information if the app crashes (such as device model, operating system version, and the technical state at the time of the crash), so we can fix bugs. This is processed by Google in line with Google's privacy policy.

StackO! requests no device permissions beyond what Google's advertising, analytics, and crash-reporting SDKs require. Your records, gems, themes, and settings are still never uploaded — the analytics and crash data above describe how the app behaves, not what you scored.

2. How Your Information Is Used

The information above is used only to:

  • Save and restore your records, gems, themes, and settings on your device.
  • Display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded advertisements that keep the game free.
  • Understand aggregate usage so we can improve the game.
  • Diagnose and fix crashes.

Uninstalling the app removes the game progress stored on your device.

3. Advertising Consent

Where required by law — for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom — StackO! shows Google's consent form before requesting personalised ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. If you were asked for consent, a AD PRIVACY OPTIONS entry appears in the in-game Settings screen so you can review or change your choice at any time.

4. Third-Party Services

StackO! uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, Google Firebase Analytics for aggregate usage insights, and Google Firebase Crashlytics for crash diagnostics. Google may collect and process data as described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not use any other advertising networks or analytics providers.

5. Children's Privacy

StackO! is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Because StackO! shows ads via Google AdMob, we rely on Google's ad settings and family policies to help keep advertising appropriate.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at legal@artivy.id.
