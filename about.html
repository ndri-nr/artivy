About Artivy

Artivy is an independent software studio from Indonesia, creating thoughtful games and useful apps for Android.

Games and apps, under one name

Artivy started with compact puzzle and arcade games. Today it also builds practical software for creators. The category can change; the standard does not. Every product should have a clear purpose, feel considered in daily use, and explain honestly how it handles data.

Artiverse is the home for Artivy's growing game collection on Android, bringing puzzles, board games, solo play, local multiplayer, achievements, themes, and leaderboards into one app. The standalone games remain available for players who prefer a focused experience, and selected titles can also be played directly in the browser.

AdLedger is the first Artivy utility app. It gives AdMob publishers a fast, read-only view of earnings and per-app performance on their phone. It cannot modify an AdMob account, and revenue data travels directly between Google and the user's device rather than through an Artivy server.

How Artivy works

Artivy is independently designed, developed, published, and supported. That keeps responsibility close to the product: the person making a design decision is also the person implementing it, reading feedback, maintaining the store listing, and fixing the result.

The goal is not to imitate a large studio. It is to make focused software with fewer layers between an idea and the person using it. New features have to earn their place through usefulness, clarity, or genuine enjoyment—not by making a screen busier.

Principles

Focused by default

A product should make its main job obvious. Games should get players into play quickly; tools should surface the information or action they were opened for.

Native where it matters

Android is the primary platform. Products are designed around the way people actually use a phone: short sessions, touch input, intermittent connections, and limited attention.

Privacy should be specific

There is no vague, shared promise standing in for product details. Every game and app has its own privacy policy describing the services it uses, the data involved, and the controls available to the user. AdLedger receives particular care because it works with private Google AdMob data and uses read-only access.

No manipulative monetisation

Artivy games do not use in-app purchases, loot boxes, paid energy, or real-money currencies. Some free games use advertising; when an optional rewarded ad is offered, the reward is stated before the user chooses to watch it. AdLedger is free and contains no ads.
