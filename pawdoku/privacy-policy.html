Privacy Policy for Pawdoku

Effective Date: July 18, 2026

Welcome to Pawdoku, a game published by Artivy. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you play Pawdoku.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways when you play Pawdoku, including:

  • Device Data: Information about your mobile device, such as device ID, model, and operating system.
  • Usage Data: In-game progress, scores, preferences, and session length.
  • Advertising Data: Data collected by third-party advertising networks to serve relevant ads.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve Pawdoku.
  • Save your game progress locally or via cloud services.
  • Serve targeted advertisements to keep the game free.
  • Analyze usage trends to improve user experience.

3. Third-Party Services

Pawdoku uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. These services may include advertising networks (e.g., AdMob, Unity Ads) and analytics providers. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies.

4. Children's Privacy

Pawdoku is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at legal@artivy.com.
