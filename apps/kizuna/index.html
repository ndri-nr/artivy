SMS gateway · Android + Web

Kizuna

Send SMS through an API, out of your own Android phones and SIM cards. Your system calls one endpoint; the message leaves from your number, on your own operator plan.

Your SIM, your numberWebhooks & schedulingNo adsIndonesia

Sign in to the portal with Google, pair a phone with a device key, then call POST /api/v1/messages. Kizuna only routes messages: it does not sell credit and the phone app never reads incoming SMS, contacts, or files.

One request, one SMS

A JSON call returns the message id at once. Status updates (sent, delivered, failed) show in the portal and arrive at your webhook, signed so you can verify them.

Many phones, one account

Pair several Android phones. Kizuna picks an online phone for each message, with per-device rate limits and clear failure reasons such as no SIM, no signal, or no credit.

Built to keep running

The phone app holds a live connection, survives reboots, and never sends the same message twice. Messages can be scheduled and cancelled before they reach the phone.
