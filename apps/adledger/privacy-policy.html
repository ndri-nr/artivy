Privacy Policy for AdLedger

Effective Date: October 29, 2026

AdLedger is a native Android app, published by Artivy, that shows you your own Google AdMob earnings as a read-only dashboard. It has no Artivy server and no account of its own: you sign in with the Google account that owns your AdMob data, and the figures are read straight from Google and shown on your phone. This policy explains exactly what AdLedger touches, where it goes, and what never leaves your device.

1. The Google account data AdLedger accesses

When you sign in, AdLedger asks Google for a single, read-only permission:

  • View your AdMob data (the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admob.readonly scope). This lets AdLedger read, and only read, your AdMob account information: the account's identifier and currency, your list of apps, and estimated-earnings and performance reports (impressions, clicks, and similar metrics) for the date ranges you pick.

AdLedger cannot change anything in your AdMob account. The scope grants no write access, so it can never create, edit, or delete your apps, ad units, payment details, or any other setting. It reads your email address only to show which account you are signed in as.

2. Where your AdMob data goes

Nowhere but your device. AdLedger has no backend: your earnings and reports are fetched directly from Google's AdMob API to your phone and displayed there. Artivy never receives, stores, or sees your revenue data.

  • Access token (in memory only): The token that authorises the read requests lives in memory while the app is running and is never written to disk. It is gone when the app closes.
  • Cached dashboard (on your device only): The last report AdLedger loaded is kept in the app's private storage so the dashboard can open without a round-trip. It is readable only by AdLedger, it is cleared when you sign out, and Android's cloud backup is switched off for the app, so this cache is never carried off the device.

Your AdMob figures are never sent to Artivy, never shared with any third party, and never used for advertising. AdLedger contains no advertising SDK and shows no ads.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy

AdLedger's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The AdMob data AdLedger reads is used only to show you your dashboard on your own device; it is not transferred to anyone else, not used for advertising, and not read by humans.

4. Crash reports

AdLedger uses Google Firebase Crashlytics for diagnostics — to receive crash reports so the app can be fixed. It sends technical crash details (device model, operating-system version, and the state at the moment of a crash).

Crash reports never carry your AdMob revenue figures, your reports, your email address, or your access token. No earnings data is ever attached to a crash report.

5. Permissions

AdLedger requests only internet and network-state access, which it needs to reach the AdMob API. It asks for no access to your contacts, files, location, camera, microphone, or any other sensitive part of the device.

6. Revoking access and removing data

  • Sign out inside AdLedger to clear the cached dashboard from your device and discard the in-memory token.
  • Revoke AdLedger's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that, AdLedger can no longer read your AdMob data.
  • Uninstall the app to remove everything it stored on your device.

Because Artivy holds none of your data, there is nothing on an Artivy server to request or delete.

7. Children's privacy

AdLedger is a tool for AdMob account holders and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are published on this page and marked by a new "Effective Date" above. Please review it periodically.
